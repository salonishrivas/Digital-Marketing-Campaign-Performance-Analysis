# Digital Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

The  **Digital Marketing Campaign Performance Analysis** project focuses on evaluating marketing campaign of Lumiere Beauty Brand performance using data-driven analysis. The goal is to understand how campaigns perform across channels, identify the factors associated with stronger engagement and conversions, and support better marketing budget and optimization decisions.

This is a portfolio project. The findings and business recommendations should be based on the actual dataset and analysis performed.

## 2. Business Problem

Marketing teams may run campaigns across multiple channels but need a consistent way to evaluate performance. Looking at clicks or impressions alone does not show whether campaigns are generating cost-effective conversions.

This project investigates questions such as:

- Which channels or campaigns generate the most conversions?
- How do impressions, clicks, and click-through rate (CTR) vary by campaign?
- Which campaigns have the lowest cost per click (CPC) or cost per acquisition (CPA)?
- How does conversion rate vary across channels, audiences, or campaign types?
- Where might marketers investigate opportunities to improve performance or allocate budget more effectively?

## 3. Project Objectives

- Measure campaign reach, engagement, conversions, and costs.
- Compare performance across channels, campaigns, and time periods.
- Calculate relevant marketing KPIs consistently.
- Identify trends, outliers, and potential areas for optimization.
- Present findings in clear charts and/or an interactive dashboard.
- Provide practical recommendations supported by the available data.

 ## 4. Data Preparation

Typical preparation steps include:

1. Inspect dataset dimensions, column names, data types, and summary statistics.
2. Check and handle missing values using a method appropriate to each field.
3. Identify duplicate records and determine whether they should be removed.
4. Validate date formats and numeric fields such as spend, clicks, and impressions.
5. Check for impossible or inconsistent values, such as negative spend or clicks exceeding impressions, where applicable.
6. Standardize campaign and channel labels.
7. Check the appropriate level of detail (for example, one row per campaign per day) before aggregating metrics.
8. Document any assumptions and exclusions.

## 5. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Use only KPIs supported by the dataset.

| KPI | Formula | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Click-Through Rate (CTR) | Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100 | Percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks |
| Cost per Click (CPC) | Spend ÷ Clicks | Average cost per click |
| Conversion Rate | Conversions ÷ Clicks × 100 | Percentage of clicks that resulted in conversions |
| Cost per Acquisition (CPA) | Spend ÷ Conversions | Average cost per conversion |
| Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) | Attributed Revenue ÷ Ad Spend | Revenue generated per unit of ad spend |
| Total Spend | Sum of Spend | Total advertising cost |
| Total Conversions | Sum of Conversions | Total recorded conversions |
| Total Revenue | Sum of Attributed Revenue | Revenue attributed to campaigns, if available |
 
## 6.Possible analyses include:

- Compare spend, clicks, conversions, and revenue by channel.
- Review CTR, conversion rate, CPC, CPA, and ROAS by campaign.
- Analyze performance trends over time.
- Identify campaigns with high spend but relatively few conversions.
- Compare engagement and conversion outcomes across available audience or campaign segments.
- Investigate outliers and data quality issues before drawing conclusions.

## 7. Dashboard / Visualizations

If you built a dashboard, consider including:

- **KPI cards:** Impressions, clicks, spend, conversions, revenue, and ROAS where available.
- **Time-series chart:** Campaign spend, conversions, or revenue over time.
- **Channel comparison:** Spend and conversions by marketing channel.
- **Campaign performance chart:** CTR, conversion rate, CPA, or ROAS by campaign.
- **Efficiency view:** Spend versus conversions or revenue.
- **Filters:** Date, channel, campaign, and audience segment, where those fields exist.
